This is the first entry of the site's development journal. It records what was set up and what follows.

## What was done

- The site source lives in `doc-vibeforma/` and is built with Astro and Starlight.
- Two languages: English at the root, Russian under `/ru/`.
- The landing pages at `/` and `/ru/` come from one component with a `locale` prop.
- Five documentation pages in both languages: introduction, installation, usage, privacy, troubleshooting.
- A blog and this journal, with the first entry in each language.
- `scripts/verify-build.mjs` builds the site and checks the required routes and the links on the landing pages.

## What comes next

The documentation grows from verified product facts, not from drafts: a page is updated when the project confirms a change. Further steps will be recorded here.
